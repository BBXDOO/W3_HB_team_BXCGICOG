# 3. EP Signal Role

EP Signal transforms repetitive binary structures into compact pulse notation.

Example:

Binary:

0011010110111000

EP:

0/221112133-8'BIN

EP focuses on:

- structural compactness
- pulse continuity
- machine parsing
- reversible transformation

EP does NOT interpret meaning.

It describes structure only.
