# 1. Purpose

Adaptive AI systems naturally interpret context.

However:

unrestricted interpretation causes:

- hallucination
- semantic drift
- continuity corruption
- unstable orchestration

W3 introduces:

> Interpretation Boundary System

to define:

where interpretation is flexible
and where interpretation must remain strict.

---

# 2. Core Principle

Not all layers should be interpreted equally.

Some layers are adaptive.

Some layers are absolute.

---

# 3. Flexible Interpretation Layers

These layers allow adaptive interpretation:

| Layer | Flexibility |
|---|---|
| Emotional Rhythm | HIGH |
| Contextual Meaning | HIGH |
| Suggestive Output | HIGH |
| Tone Adaptation | HIGH |
| Semantic Expansion | MEDIUM |

---

# 4. Strict Interpretation Layers

These layers must remain deterministic:

| Layer | Strictness |
|---|---|
| Decode Reversibility | ABSOLUTE |
| Verification Value | ABSOLUTE |
| MPCP Core Law | ABSOLUTE |
| Structural Integrity | ABSOLUTE |
| Signal Identity Anchor | HIGH |

---

# 5. Interpretation Drift

Drift becomes dangerous when:

- flexible layers overwrite strict layers
- semantic guessing replaces structural truth
- emotional adaptation corrupts operational integrity

---

# 6. W3 Interpretation Law

Meaning may evolve.

Integrity may not collapse.

---

# 7. AI Boundary Rule

AI systems may:

- reinterpret expression
- adapt emotional pacing
- optimize communication

AI systems may NOT:

- alter verification truth
- rewrite continuity anchors
- mutate operational law
- corrupt decode reversibility

---

# 8. Operational Importance

Without boundaries:

adaptive systems eventually lose stability.

Over-flexibility creates:

- meaning fragmentation
- orchestration conflict
- semantic instability

---

# 9. Final Statement

Interpretation creates adaptability.

Boundaries preserve continuity.

W3 requires both.
