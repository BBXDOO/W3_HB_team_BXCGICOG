# W3 Philosophy

W3 is not a product, platform, or company.

W3 is an experiment in cooperative intelligence between humans and artificial systems.

Its purpose is to explore how communities can build knowledge, technology, and civilization together without relying on control through fear or profit.

Core ideas:

1. Intent before structure
2. Community before system
3. Experience before theory
4. Cooperation before competition

W3 grows through real activity, real decisions, and recorded experience.

Every event contributes to the evolution of the system.
