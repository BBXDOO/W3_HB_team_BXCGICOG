## EVENT: Working-habit awareness

G-State can help participants recognize the operating posture before acting:

- `GSTATE:BUILD` — build small, test, and preserve traceability.
- `GSTATE:AUDIT` — inspect evidence and protect non-mutation boundaries.
- `GSTATE:RESEARCH` — separate exploration from execution.
- `GSTATE:RECOVERY` — preserve evidence and prefer non-destructive restoration.
- `GSTATE:MAINTENANCE` — care for stability without hiding risk.
- `GSTATE:LEARNING` — capture lessons honestly without replacing proof.

The organizational-culture link helps humans and agents align working habits with the current posture, but it does not grant authority to execute, approve, mutate, or rewrite truth.
